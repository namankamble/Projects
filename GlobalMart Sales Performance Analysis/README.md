# ***GlobalMart Sales Performance Analysis***

A Power BI business intelligence project analyzing historical sales data from **GlobalMart** to uncover actionable insights for increasing profitability, optimizing regional and product performance, and understanding customer behavior.

---

## ***Business Objectives:***
- Improve overall **profit margin by at least 5%** in the upcoming quarter.
- Identify **top 3 most profitable** and **bottom 3 least profitable** product categories.
- Analyze **sales and profit by region** to find underperforming areas and leverage top-performing ones.
- Segment **customers based on sales contribution and profitability** for targeted marketing.
- Evaluate **discount impact** on sales and profit to refine promotional strategies.

---

## ***Key Deliverables:***
- Executive Summary Dashboard with:
  - Total Sales
  - Total Profit
  - Profit Margin over time (Year, Quarter, Month)
- Sales & Profit breakdown:
  - By Region (Map & Bar Chart)
  - By Product Category and Product Name (with drill-down)
- Customer Insights:
  - Sales & Profit by Customer Segment
  - Top 10 Customers by Sales and Profit
  - Unique Customers and Orders count
- Order & Shipping Analysis:
  - Average Shipping Duration (ShipDate - OrderDate)
  - Shipping Mode usage and its impact on Sales/Profit
- Discount & Payment Method Analysis:
  - Discount distribution and correlation with Profit
  - Most frequently used Payment Methods and related Sales

---

## ***Tools & Skills Used:***
- **Tools:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Python, Plotly
- **Skills:** KPI Design, Drill-Down Dashboards, Customer Segmentation, Sales Analysis

---

## ***Outcome:***
Delivered an interactive and visually engaging Power BI report that helped the GlobalMart leadership make informed, data-driven decisions regarding pricing, promotions, inventory, and regional strategy.
